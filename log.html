What the cost means

Estimated discarded-product cost is the discarded quantity multiplied by your entered production cost. Missing costs stay unknown. These totals do not claim lost revenue or savings already achieved.

Columns and values

Required for every row: record_date as YYYY-MM-DD, store, product and kind.

Use finished_product with produced and sold, or production_waste with waste_kg. Leave fields for the other type blank.

Read the past

Use the store’s earlier sales and known calendar information. Missing dates stay missing; they are never treated as zero sales.

02
Predict a whole week

The baseline repeats the same day from the previous week. The ML model learns from earlier patterns. Both forecasts are issued before their target week starts.

03
Compare with what happened

MAE means average absolute error. Lower values mean closer predictions. Both methods are measured on the same dates in the final 56-day test period.
